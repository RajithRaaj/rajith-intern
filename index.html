Aloe Rauhii Care

☀️

Sunlight

Prefers bright, indirect light. A windowsill with morning sun is the perfect spot. Avoid harsh, direct afternoon sun which can scorch its delicate leaves.

💧

Watering

Allow the soil to dry completely out between waterings. When you do water, soak it thoroughly until water runs out the bottom. Less is more.

🪴

Soil & Potting

Needs excellent drainage. A cactus or succulent soil mix is ideal. Only repot when it outgrows its current container, usually every 2-3 years.

🌡️

Environment

Thrives in standard indoor room temperatures (65°F - 80°F). Keep away from cold drafts or AC vents. It does not require high humidity.
